This is the first post on a new site. It is a sample, so replace it with your own words.

The plan is simple. I will write short notes about things I build, things that broke, and things I learned while fixing them. Some posts will be long. Most will not.

## What to expect

- Notes on tools and systems I use every day.
- Write-ups of side projects, including the ones that failed.
- The occasional opinion, clearly marked as one.

> Write what you would want to find when you search for it at 2 a.m.
