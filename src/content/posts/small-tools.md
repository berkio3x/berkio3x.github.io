Most of the tools I still use after many years are small. They do one job, they start fast, and they do not need a manual.

## Three tests

Before I build a tool, I ask three questions.

1. Can I explain what it does in one sentence?
2. Will it still work if I forget about it for a year?
3. Can someone else delete it without asking me?

If the answer to all three is yes, I build it. If not, I make it smaller first.
